| Hybridisation                            | Homogenisation                    |
| :--------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Cultures mix to form something new       | Cultures become more similar      |
| Blending of local + global influences    | Spread of dominant global culture |

# Hybridisation 
## Definition 
- Process in which foreign and local cultures are blended, resulting in unique combinations of both elements to produce new cultural products
- e.g. Fusion Foods such as Nasi Lemak burger, Prosperity burger, Laksa Pasta
# Homogenisation
## Definition
- Process in which local cultures are altered by the foreign cultures to become more similar to the foreign cultures
- Resulting in the dominance of the foreign culture
- e.g. Shared global media culture - Hollywood films globally
- Global Dominance of Western fast food chains - Starbucks, Mcdonalds
