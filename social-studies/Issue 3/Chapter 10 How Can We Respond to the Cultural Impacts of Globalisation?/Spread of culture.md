# Definition
- Popular and consumer culture can influence people's way of life and identities due to its spread globally, and many people continue to embrace it as they find it familiar and relatable
# Examples
| Case Studies                               | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| American influences on fast-food and media | Many American fast foods chains have opened up in many different countries around the world, and have become intertwined with local culture. For example, more than 300 people visited the McDonald's outlet at King Albert Park to bid it farewell on its final day of operations on 16 March 2014. For 23 years, it was a popular gathering spot for students, and many lamented the loss of this memorable place and shared stories about the impact this fast-food outlet had on their lives. |
| Korean Wave                                | An example of cultural globalisation is the Korean Wave, or Hallyu, from South Korea. Since the mid-2000s, Korean influences have spread through beauty products, television dramas, electronics, food and Korean pop music                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
