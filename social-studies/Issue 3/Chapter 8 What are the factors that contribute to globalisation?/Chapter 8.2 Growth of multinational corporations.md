# Definition
- MNCs are large corporations that operate in various countries, with supply chains that are global in scope linking numerous people and activities across different places in the world
# Examples
| Case Studies     | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| :--------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Starbucks Coffee | When Starbucks first opened, they travelled to the source to get coffee beans. They source their coffee beans from more than 30 countries across Latin America, Africa and Asia. Beans are transported to different roasting facilities in different cities in the United States and India. Warehouses then pack and store roasted coffee for distribution to retailers. The roasted coffee is then distributed to more than 35000 stores around the world |
# Explanation
- The growth of multinational companies has led to movement of people, goods and services across the globe. When MNCs set up subsidiaries in other countries, they increase the interconnections between people, activities and goods throughout the world, and facilitate exchange of ideas between countries, driving globalisation as more interdependent and interconnected relationships are established.
