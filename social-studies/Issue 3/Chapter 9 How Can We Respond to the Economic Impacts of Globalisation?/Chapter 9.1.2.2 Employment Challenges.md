# Definition
- Globalisation can lead to economic challenges in individuals' employment, as MNCs may relocate their operations to other countries to save costs or access expertise available elsewhere, and individuals who are employed in these operations may lose their jobs
# Examples

| Case Studies | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Amazon       | Amazon laid off 18000 jobs between Novemeber 2022 and January 2023, including those in advertising, gaming and cloud services. Many units in Amazon expanded their manpower rapidly during the pandemic due to high demand for digital services, but the slowing growth lead to Amazon reducing their workforce to cut costs. |
