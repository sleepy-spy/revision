# Definition
- Decisions on how to address the various needs and interest of people may lead to varying impact on different groups of people. While a decision may benefit society in general, it can have negative impact on certain groups of people. This complexity makes it challenging to decide what is good for society
# Examples
| Case Study                                                   | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Building foreign workers' dormitories near residential areas | On 1 June 2020, the Ministry of National Development and the Ministry of Manpower announced that more dormitories would be built to improve the living standards of workers in the construction sector. Because of Singapore's land constraints, some dormitories would be located near residential areas, and the news was met with mixed reactions from Singaporeans. |
