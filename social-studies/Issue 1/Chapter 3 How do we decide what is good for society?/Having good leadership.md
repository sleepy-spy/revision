# Definition
- Having good leadership means that the government has leaders who are capable, honest and incorruptible
# Examples

| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mr Lim Kim San | He was the first chairman of the Housing and Development Board and became Minister for National Development in 1963. From 1960, he led the construction of low-cost public housing to tackle the housing shortage. In slightly over three years, HDB managed to complete 26000 flats, and was considered an exceptional achievement given that the Singapore Improvement Trust took over 32 years to complete 23019 units. |
