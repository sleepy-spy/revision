# Definition
- A trade-off involves making a decision between two or more things that cannot be obtained at the same time due to limited resources. This means that have more one one thing would result in having less of another

| Cross-Island Line | In January 2013, the Singapore government announced plans to build the Cross-Island Line (CRL) as Singapore's eighth Mass Rapid Transit Line, and was proposed the run under the Central Catchment Nature Reserve. However, in June 2013, the Nature Society (Singapore) raise concerns about the CRL construction, and presented its views on how the CRL would affect habitats within the Central Catchment Nature Reserve, and recommended adjusting the route to skirt around, rather than passing under the Nature Reserve. |
