## Purpose
- Targets false statements of fact spread online in Singapore
- Aims to protect public interest and reduce fake news
- Does NOT apply to opinions, satire, criticism, or parody
## Enforcement measures
### 1. Correction Direction
- Offender must keep original post
- Must add correction notice
- Notice explains falsehood + provides correct facts
### 2. Stop Communication Direction
- More serious cases
- Offender must remove the false post entirely
### 3. Declared Online Locations
- Sites repeatedly spreading falsehoods (≥3 within 6 months)
- Can be blacklisted by government
- Not allowed to profit from spreading false information
## Penalties
- Fine and/or imprisonment for offenders
