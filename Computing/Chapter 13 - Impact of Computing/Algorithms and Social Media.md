# Engagement Rate (Social Media Algorithms)
- Engagement rate = likes, shares, comments used to rank/promote posts in feeds
## Reasons for use:
### 1. Improve relevance of content
- Shows users posts they are more likely to find interesting
- Increases usefulness and time spent on platform
### 2. Incentivise content creation
- Creators rewarded with higher visibility for engaging posts
- Encourages production of higher-quality / more interesting content
### 3. Increase advertising revenue
- High engagement → more user attention
- Allows platforms to charge higher ad rates for popular content areas
