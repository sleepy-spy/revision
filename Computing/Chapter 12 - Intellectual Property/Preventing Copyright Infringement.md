| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Check and follow the website’s terms and conditions.                             | Websites often have rules about using their content, like whether you can copy, download, or link to materials. Some content may have a specific license, like a CC license, that states if you can use it or not. If there are no notices, you should ask the copyright owners for permission. |
| Limit reproduction of a copyrighted work to 10% (for educational purposes only). | If you are using copyrighted materials for research, study, or other educational purposes, you can copy up to 10% of the electronic version of a work. The copied work should not be changed and must be properly cited to avoid plagiarism                                                     |
| Consider using public domain material instead                                    | Works found in the public domain can be copied and modified without permission. However, it is still important to acknowledge or cite the authors properly to avoid plagiarism.                                                                                                                 |
| Verify and cite your sources when using generative AI.                           | It is important to be aware that generative AI can create inaccurate and potentially plagiarised content. Therefore, it is crucial to acknowledge your use of generative AI, and to verify and cite the sources used by the AI to generate the content.                                         |
