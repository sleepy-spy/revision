# Client-Server Network
## Client
- Computer that initiates a connection to a server to request for resources and services to perform operations
## Server
- Computer that shares resources and responds to requests from devices and other servers on the network. 
- Usually higher capacity and is more powerful than a client as it needs to manage resources and services
