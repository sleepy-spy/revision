- The turning effect of a force is called a moment or a torque
- A force can produce a clockwise moment, anticlockwise moment or no moment at all
# Definition
- The moment of a force is the product of the force $F$ and the perpendicular distance $d_{\perp}$ from the pivot to the line of action of the force
	- moment = $F \times d_{\perp}$
- It is a vector quantity
