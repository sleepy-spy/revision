- When an object is in equilibrium
	- the resultant force acting on it is zero (translational equilibrium)
	- the resultant moment acting on it is zero (rotational equilibrium)
# Definition
- The principle of moments states that when a body is in rotational equilibrium, the sum of clockwise moments is equal to the sum of anticlockwise moments about the same pivot
	- $\text{Sum of CW Moments} = \text{Sum of ACW Moments}$
