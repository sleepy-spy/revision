- Waves follow the law of reflection: the angle of incidence, $i$, is equal to the angle of reflection, $r$
- Waves undergo refraction when
	- they travel across a boundary where the speed on one side differs from the speed on the other side
	- The angle of incidence at the boundary is nonzero
## What happens to water wave when it crosses from deep region to shallow region
- The speed of the wave, $v$, decreases
- The frequency of the wave remains unchanged since the time it was produced from the source
- Since $v = f\lambda$, the wavelength, $\lambda$ of the wave decreases, and the wavefronts become more closely spaced
- The wave bends towards the normal, and the angle of refraction, $r$, is smaller than the angle of incidence, $i$
- The wavefronts are connected at the interface
$$f_{\mathrm{shallow}} = f_{\mathrm{deep}}$$
$$\therefore \frac{v_{\mathrm{shallow}}}{\lambda_{\mathrm{shallow}}} = \frac{v_{\mathrm{deep}}}{\lambda{\mathrm{deep}}}$$
