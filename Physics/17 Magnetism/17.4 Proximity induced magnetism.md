- **Induced magnetism** can take place when a magnetic materials becomes a magnet when it is near or in contact with a strong magnet or within a current-carrying solenoid
# What gives rise to magnetic properties?
- Magnets are made of atoms
- The movement and arrangement of electrons in the atoms give rise to an atomic magnet
- A **magnetic domain** consists of a group of atomic magnets pointing in the same direction
![[Screenshot 2026-04-04 at 11.06.04 AM.png]]
- The magnetic domains in an **unmagnetised material** point in random directions
# How do magnets induce magnetism?
- When a bar magnet is brought near, the material is magnetised by induced magnetism
