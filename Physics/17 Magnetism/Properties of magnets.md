# Properties
-  A freely suspended magnets comes to rest in the north-south direction.
![[Screenshot 2026-04-04 at 9.21.04 AM.png]]
- A magnet has two poles, the **north pole** (N) and the **south pole** (S)
	- Magnetic forces are **non-contact** forces.
- The magnetic field is always **strongest** at the **poles**, which are located a short distance away from the physical ends of the magnet
- The poles of a magnet always comes in pairs
	- When a magnet is divided into two smaller pieces, they each become a magnet with a north and south pole
