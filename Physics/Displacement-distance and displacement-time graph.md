## Displacement-distance graph
- A displacement-distance graph describes displacements of points at different distances along the wave at a **fixed instant of time**
- The amplitude, $A$, can also be determined with a displacement-distance graph
## Displacement-time graph
- The displacement-time graphs describes the displacement of one point (located at a fixed distance) **at different times**
- One cycle along the horizontal axis represents the period $T = \frac{1}{F}$
- The amplitude, $A$, can also be determined with a displacement-distance graph
