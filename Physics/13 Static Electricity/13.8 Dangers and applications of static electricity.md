# Applications
- **Spray painting**
	- charged paint droplets repel each other, attract the surface, resulting in even coating and reduced pain waste
- **Electrostatic precipitator**
	- particles gain charge, get attracted to plates, and the air is cleaned
- **Van de Graaff generator**
	- dome accumulates charge, repels hair
- **Electroscope**
	- detects nearby charges, causing the gold leaves to repel 
# Dangers
- **Charge accumulation**: Large amounts of charge build up.
    - nearby electrons may rush in to neutralise it (Positive Charge)
    - excess electrons repel each other strongly (Negative Charge)
- **Sudden discharge**: Rapid movement of electrons causes sparks.
    - sparks near flammable substances causes fires or explosions
