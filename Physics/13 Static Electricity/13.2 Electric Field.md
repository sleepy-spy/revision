# What it is
- An electric field is a region in which an electric charge experiences an electric force
# Properties of Electric Fields
- The direction of an electric field at a location is the direction of the net electric that would act on a small positive test charge if placed at that location
- The stronger the electric field at a location, the more closely spaced the electric field lines at that location
- Electric field lines point away from positive source charges, and point toward negative source charges.
- Electric field lines never cross one another, because if it did, a small positive test charge placed at that intersection would experience two net electric forces in different directions, which is impossible
