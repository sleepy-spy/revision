## How it occurs
- Due to friction, electrons may be transferred from electrically neutral insulator to another electrically neutral insulator when they rub against each other
- Only atoms on the surface lose and gain electrons 
	- Surface atoms for the electron transferrer becomes cations, and surface atoms for the electron receiver becomes anions
## Can electrically neutral conductors be charged friction?
- No, any excess charges on the conductors are quickly neutralised
- If there are any excess free electrons on one conductor, the electrons repel into the second conductor, or move through the hand and into the earth and leaving the first conductor electrically neutral.
