# Types of Charges
- Electron
	- Charge of $-1.6 \cdot 10^{-19}C$
- Proton
	- Charge of $1.6 \cdot 10^{-19}C$
- Neutrons
	- No charge
# Ions
| Particle | Net Charge           | Number of electrons vs number of protons                       |
| :------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Anion    | Net positive charge  | has excess electrons (compared to the number of protons)       |
| Atom     | Electrically neutral | has an equal number of electrons and protons                   |
| Anion    | Net negative charge  | has a deficit of electrons (compared to the number of protons) |
# Law of conservation of charge
- The net charge of a closed system remains unchanged
# Direction of electric force
- 2 objects with like charges exert repulsive electric forces on each other
- 2 objects with unlike charges exert attractive electric forces on each other
- By Newton's 3rd law, if charged object A exerts an electric force on charged object B, B also exerts an electric force on charged object A of the same magnitude in the opposite direction. This is true even if A and B have different amounts of charge.
