## Waveform
- The shape of the wave
## Crests
- The highest points on a transverse wave
	- A particle at a crest is momentarily at rest
## Troughs
- The lowest points on a transverse wave
	- A particle at a trough is momentarily at rest
## Compressions
- Compressed regions in a longitudinal wave with a greater pressure where particles are displaced so that they are closer together on average
	- The pressure is maximum at the **centre of compression**
## Rarefactions
- Rarefied regions in a longitudinal wave with a lower pressure where particles are displaced so that they are further apart on average
	- The pressure is minimum at the **centre of rarefaction**
## Displacement
- The vector from the equilibrium position to a point on the wave
	- It's magnitude is equal to the straight-line distance between the equilibrium position to that point
	- SI Unit: metre (m)
## Amplitude, $A$
- The maximum magnitude of displacement of a point in the wave from its equilibrium position
## Wavelength, $\lambda$
- The shortest distance between two successive points with the same phase
	- SI Unit: metre(m)
## Period, $T$
- The time taken by each point on the wave to complete one oscillation
	- SI Unit: second (s)
## Frequency, $f$
- The number of oscillations each point completes per unit time
	- SI Unit: Hertz (Hz), 1 Hz = 1 cycle per second
- The frequency is equal to the reciprocal of the period
$$f = \frac{1}{T}$$
## In phase
- 2 or more points of a wave are in phase if they always have the same
	- direction of displacement from their equilibrium positions and
	- direction of motion
- The shortest distance between two successive points that are in phase is the wavelength, $\lambda$
- It is completely out of phase if the distance between the two points are an integer + half $\lambda$ apart.
## Wavefront
- An imaginary line that joins adjacent points that are in phase
	- It is perpendicular to the direction of wave travel
