| Factor                       | Transverse Waves                                                                                                    | Longitudinal Waves                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Description                  | Transverse waves are waves that have a direction of vibration that is perpendicular to the direction of wave travel | Longitudinal waves are waves that have a direction of vibration that is parallel to the direction of wave travel |
| Examples                     | • Waves on a rope<br>• Water waves in a ripple tank<br>• Electromagnetic waves<br>• Earthquake S waves              | • Sound Waves<br>• Earthquake P waves                                                                            |
| Direction of Energy Transfer | Same as direction of wave travel                                                                                    | Same as direction of wave travel                                                                                 |
