## Definition
- The speed, $v$, of a wave is the distance travelled by the wave per unit time
$$v = \frac{d}{t}$$
- where
	- v is speed of the wave
	- d is the distance travelled by the wave
	- t is time taken
- In a time interval $T$, the wave travels a distance of one wavelength, $\lambda$
- We can then derive
$$ v = \frac{\lambda}{T}$$
$$ v= \frac{1}{T}\ \lambda$$
$$v = f\lambda$$
- where
	- f is the frequency of the wave
	- $\lambda$ is the wavelength of the wave
