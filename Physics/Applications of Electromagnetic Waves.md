
| EM Radiation Type    | Key Properties                                                                    | Common Uses                                                                              |
| :------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Radio Waves**      | Longer wavelengths; penetrate obstructions better than microwaves.                | TV/Radio communication, Navigation systems, Radar, RFID tags.                            |
| **Microwaves**       | Penetrates haze, rain, clouds, and snow.                                          | Satellite TV, Microwave ovens (vibrates water molecules; passes through glass/plastic).  |
| **Infrared (IR)**    | Felt as heat; works in darkness.                                                  | TV remotes, Ear thermometers, Intruder alarms.                                           |
| **Visible Light**    | Visible to the human eye; travels through glass fibres.                           | Optical fibres (telecoms), Endoscopes, Lasers (eye surgery, cutting steel).              |
| **Ultraviolet (UV)** | High energy; can kill bacteria.                                                   | Sterilising medical equipment, Sunbeds (tanning), Banknote forgery detection.            |
| **X-Rays**           | Penetrates most materials (except lead); produced by high-energy electron impact. | Bone fracture/tooth decay imaging, Airport security, Crystal structure analysis.         |
| **Gamma Rays**       | Highest energy; emitted from radioactive decay or nuclear reactions.              | Medical tracers (I-131), Radiation therapy (killing tumors), Surgical tools (Cobalt-60). |
