## Accuracy vs Precision
| Accuracy                                                                          | Precision                                                                   |
| :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Accuracy refers to the closeness of a measured value to a standard or known value | Precision refers to the closeness of two or more measurements to each other |
