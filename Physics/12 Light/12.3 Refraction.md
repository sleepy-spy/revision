## Definition
- Refraction is the **bending** of light as it travels from one optical medium to another with a different refractive index
- It is called by the change in the speed of light as light travels from one medium to another
## Important Angles and Lines
![[Screenshot 2026-02-15 at 7.10.33 PM.png]]
- Normal is an imaginary straight line that is perpendicular (90°) to the surface of the medium.
- Angle of incidence ($i$) is the angle between the incident ray and the normal.
- Angle of refraction ($r$) is the angle between the refracted ray and the normal.
- Remember the weak reflected ray when drawing diagrams!
- The incident ray, refracted ray and the normal all lie in the same plane
## Refractive Index
- The Refractive Index is defined as the ratio of speed of light in a vacuum and the speed of light in the medium
 $$n = \frac{c}{v}$$
- Where 
	- n is the refractive index
	- c is the speed of light in a vacuum ($3.0 * 10^8 m/s$)
	- v is the speed of light in the medium
- It is medium specific
- Usually, the refractive index from slowest to fastest:
	- Solid -> Liquid -> Gas -> Vacuum
## Snell's Law of Refraction
- For any two given media, the ratio of the sine of the angle of incidence to the sine of the angle of refraction is a constant
- That is
$$n_i\sin(i) = n_r\sin(r)$$
	$$\mathrm{and}$$
	$$\frac{\sin(i)}{\sin(r)} = \frac{n_r}{n_i}\ \mathrm{(a\ constant)}$$

- where
	- $n_i$ = refractive index of the medium that the incident ray travels in
	- $n_r$ = refractive index of the medium that the refracted ray travels in
## Effects of Refraction
### Misperception of Depth
- In a medium with refractive index > 1, the apparent depth is **less than** the real depth
$$\mathrm{Refractive\ Index},\ n = \frac{\mathrm{real\ depth}}{\mathrm{apparent\ depth}} $$
### Chromatic Dispersion
- The colour of light depends on the frequency of the light wave
- **Dispersion** is the phenomenon where different frequencies of light travel at different speeds through a medium.
- The refractive index of the prism glass depends on frequency (colour). Light rays of each colour travels through the prism at a different speed.
- At the surfaces of the prism, light rays of each colour is refracted by a different angle.
- The emergent rays splits into a range of colours (spectrum).
