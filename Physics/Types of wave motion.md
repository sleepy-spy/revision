## Transverse Waves
- Transverse waves are waves that have a direction of vibration that is perpendicular to the direction of wave travel
### Examples of Transverse Waves
- Waves on a rope
- Water waves in a ripple tank
- Electromagnetic waves
- Earthquake S waves
## Longitudinal Waves
- Longitudinal waves are waves that have a direction of vibration that is parallel to the direction of wave travel
### Examples of Longitudinal Waves
- Sound Waves
- Earthquake P waves
## Direction of Energy Transfer
- For both waves, the direction of energy transfer is the same as the direction of wave travel
