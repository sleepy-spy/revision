- Human ears can hear sounds from 20Hz to 20000Hz, and this range is called the audible range of frequencies.
- A sound with a frequency lower than 20Hz, the lower limit of the human audible range of frequencies, is known as infrasound
- A sound with a frequency higher than 20000Hz, the upper limit of the human audible range of frequencies, is know as ultrasound
## Applications of ultrasound
- Ultrasound can be used to break apart kidney stones, as its high intensity vibrations can break the stone into smaller pieces to be expelled through urine
- Ultrasound can also be used to kill cancer cells and tumours, as it is safe and non-invasive
