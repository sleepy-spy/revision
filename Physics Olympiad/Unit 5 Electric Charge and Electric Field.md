## Coulomb's Law
$$\mathbf{F} = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\, \frac{q q_0}{r^2}\, \hat{\mathbf{r}}$$
$$\mathrm{Where\ \epsilon_0\ is\ the\ permittivity\ of\ free\ space,\ and\ equal\ to}$$
$$\epsilon_0 = 8.854 \times 10^{-12}\, N\, m^2\, C^{-2}$$ 

## Electric Field
$$\mathbf{E} = \lim_{q_0 \to 0}\frac{\mathbf{F}_0}{q_0}$$
$$\mathbf{F}_0 = q_0\, \mathbf{E}$$
$$\mathbf{E} = \frac{\mathbf{F}_0}{q_0}$$
$$\mathbf{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\, \frac{q}{r^2}\, \hat{\mathbf{r}}$$

## Electric Flux
$$\boldsymbol{\phi}_E = \mathbf{E} \cdot \mathbf{A}$$
