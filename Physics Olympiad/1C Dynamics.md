## Newton's First Law
$$\mathrm{If}\ \sum{\vec{F}} = 0,\ \mathrm{\vec{a}}=0\ \mathrm{and}\ \vec{v}\ \mathrm{is\ constant}$$
## Newton's Second Law
$$\sum{\vec{F}} = m\vec{a}$$
## Mass and Weight
$$\vec{w} = m\vec{g}$$
## Newton's Third Law
$$\vec{F}_{\mathrm{A\ on\ B}} = -\vec{F}_{\mathrm{B\ on\ A}}$$
