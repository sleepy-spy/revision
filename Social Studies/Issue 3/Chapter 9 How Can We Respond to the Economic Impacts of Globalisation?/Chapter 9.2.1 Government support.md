# Definition
- Countries may cooperate with other countries by pursuing policies that promote free trade. They may also attract and make foreign investments to sustain their economic growth. Countries also provide financial support to cushion negative economic impacts and set up agencies to provide resources for businesses to innovate, develop expertise and even go abroad

| European Union (EU)-Singapore Free Trade Agreement | The European Union (EU)-Singapore Free Trade Agreement came into effect in November 2019. Under the agreement, the EU would remove tariffs on 84 percent of all Singapore products entering the EU within the first year and the remaining 16 percent over a period of three to five years. It also enhanced market access for Asian food products made in Singapore.  |
