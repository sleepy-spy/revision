# Definition
- A country experiences economic growth when it increases its gross domestic product over a period of time because it produces more goods and services.
# Examples
| Case Studies       | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Singapore's Growth | After gaining independence in 1965, Singapore faced high unemployment rate. The government sought to attract foreign investments in response, and by the end of the 1960s to the early 1970s, Singapore enjoyed strong economic growth, and Singaporeans had more jobs and higher wages. In the 2010s to 2020s, Singapore continues to attract Foreign Direct Investments with major sources from China, Japan and the United States. By 2018, 59 per cent of the total number of technology-based MNCs in Asia had established regional headquarters in Singapore |
# Explanation
- As countries participate in the global economy through international trade, this enables their goods and services to reach a larger market, allowing goods to not just be sold locally but to other countries, increasing their production to meet market demand. Countries also experience economic growth as a result of foreign investments, which bring in capital and advanced technology
