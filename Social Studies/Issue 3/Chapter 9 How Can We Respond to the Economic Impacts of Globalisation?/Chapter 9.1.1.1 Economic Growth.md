# Definition
- As countries participate in the global economy through international trade, this enables their goods and services to reach a larger market, allowing goods to not just be sold locally but to other countries, increasing their production to meet market demand. Countries also experience economic growth as a result of foreign investments, which bring in capital and advanced technology

| :---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Singapore 1965 to early 1970s | After Singapore gained independent, the government sought to attract foreign investments, especially in labour intensive industries. By the end of the 1960s to the early 1970s, Singapore enjoyed strong economic growth, and Singaporeans had more jobs and higher wages |
