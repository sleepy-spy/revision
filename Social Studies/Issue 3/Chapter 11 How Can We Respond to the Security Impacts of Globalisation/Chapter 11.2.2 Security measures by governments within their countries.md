# Definition 
- Transnational terrorism: Countries can implement border-management measures to prevent potential terrorist attacks from occurring within their country
- Cyber threats: Set up dedicated agencies with the expertise and resources needed to investigate and handle cyber threats
# Examples
| Case Study                         | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Border-management measures         | Countries can implement technological innovations at land and sea borders, such as body and baggage scanners and x-ray imaging equipment. They can also use biometrics to verify identity of individuals at checkpoints, such as using fingerprints or facial features.                                                                 |
| Cyber Security Agency of Singapore | The Cyber Security Agency of Singapore was established in 2015 to oversee and coordinate all aspects of cybersecurity for the nation. They also outlined Singapore's goals in the Singapore Cybersecurity Strategy 2021 document, with plans such as building more resilient infrastructure and growing a robust cyber talent pipeline. |
