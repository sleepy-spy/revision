# Definition
 - Transnational terrorism: Countries can share information on terrorsm threats and participate in counter-terrorism cooperation
 - Cyber threats: Countries can participate in cooperation to tackle cyber threats
# Example
| Case Study                                    | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| INTERPOL                                      | The International Criminal Police Organization is an inter-governmental organisation that works with 196 countries to improve world safety. In 2019, an officer from the Singapore Police Force was appointed lead INTERPOL's Regional Counter-Terrorism Node, which enables police units in the Asia-Pacific region to work together and transfer intelligence concerning terrorist activities |
| Asia Pacific Computer Emergency Response Team | The Asia Pacific Computer Emergency Response Team promotes regional cooperation on information security in the Asia-Pacific region, and also shares cyber threat data to countries in the region. They also maintain a cooperative network of computer security experts in the region                                                                                                           |
