| IMDA Concert Cancellation | In 2019, the Infocomm Media Development Authority cancelled a schedule live concert by an overseas band following an online petition. They had initially allowed the concert, imposing strict requirements such as not allowing the band to refer to religion or use religious symbols in its performances. However, they assessed that it should not proceed due to the band's past performances promoting violence, hateful sentiments and religiously offensive content |
| Naatu Naatu               | A dance cover of an award-winning Indian song, "Naatu Naatu" by South Korean diplomats in India went viral on social media. It was the first time South Korea's Ministry of Foreign Affairs had published such a viral video, which garnered a warm response in India.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
