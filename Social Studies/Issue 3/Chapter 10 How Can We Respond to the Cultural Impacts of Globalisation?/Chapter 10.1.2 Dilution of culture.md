# Definition
- Process in which local cultures are altered by the foreign cultures to become more similar to the foreign cultures, resulting in the dominance of the foreign culture
# Examples
| Case Studies          | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Singapore Music Scene | In 1984, the first Xinyao album was released to overwhelming success. However, the rise rise of international megastars like Jacky Cheung and Andy Lau in the 1990s overshadowed Xinyao, and radio stations felt Singaporeans preferred commercial releases in other countries over local compositions. Until today, most youths prefer listening to Taylor Swift and Adam Levine rather than Singaporean singers like JJ Lin and Stefanie Sun |
