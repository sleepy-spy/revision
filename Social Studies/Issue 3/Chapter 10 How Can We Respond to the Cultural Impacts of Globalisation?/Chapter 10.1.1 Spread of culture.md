# Definition
- Globalisation has led to the spread of popular and consumer culture and the consumer lifestyle associated with it. 
# Examples
| Case Studies                               | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| American influences on fast-food and media | Many American fast foods chains have opened up in many different countries around the world. For example, McDonald's has the most amount of locations around the world for a fast-food chain at over 38700 locations around the world as of 2022.                                 |
| Korean Wave                                | Since the mid-2000s, Korean influences have spread through beauty products, television dramas, electronics, food and Korean pop music, showing that the spread of culture does not always come from Western societies, and can come from any part of the world with globalisation |
