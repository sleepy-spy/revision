# Definition 
- The Legislative makes and passes by passing a bill which is a draft debated in Parliament, and becomes the law after the President approves of the law
# Examples
| Case Studies                      | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| COVID-19 (Temporary Measures) Act | In April 2020, amid the growing threat of COVID-19 in Singapore, the government passed the COVID-19 (Temporary Measures) Act that made the wearing of masks compulsory by law. It was passed to control the transmission of the virus in Singapore. |
