# Definition
- The Executive implements and enforces laws by formulating and implementing government policies in areas such as education, healthcare, housing and transport, as well as day-to-day administration of the state. They also get support from various ministries and statutory boards.
# Examples
| Case Study                        | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| COVID-19 (Temporary Measures) Act | Several government agencies were issued a letter of appointment by the Ministry of Health and involved in enforcing the COVID-19 (Temporary Measures) Act. Officers from these agencies can issue a Notice of Offence, which are fines and details of offence. The fine is $300 for first-time offenders, and $1000 for repeat offenders. Thousands of Safe Distancing Ambassadors were also deployed to remind the public to abide by the regulations and ensure masks were worn correctly |
