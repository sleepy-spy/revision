# Definition
- The government ensures the economic and well-being of citizens such that people's basic needs for food, water and shelter are met, and they have the ability to pursue their goals and enjoy a satisfactory quality of life. The government does this by providing goods and services to the public at no cost or highly subsidised for increased accessibility to citizens

| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Central Provident Fund (CPF) | In Singapore, the Central Provident Fund is a comprehensive savings plan that provides a foundation for retirement through the CPF Special Account. It can be also used to buy a home, pay for insurance or grow savings through investments using the CPF Ordinary Account. CPF can also help you pay for healthcare expenses and insurance through the Medisave Account. |
