# Definition
- Addressing the needs of society involves responding to issues and challenges facing different groups in society. These challenges faced by certain groups are complex and people could benefit from more personalised attention and care.
# Examples

| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mdm Malligesvari                   | Through RSVP Singapore, Mdm Malligesvari identified areas of need in the community that she was passionate and concerned about. She volunteers as an instructor for the Age Well Everyday programme. and teaches senior citizens how to stay healthy, exercise and keep active through brain-stimulating activities.                                                                                                                                     |
| RSVP Singapore                     | RSVP Singapore was formed in 1998 and actively engages senior citizens in volunteerism, and provides seniors with meaningful opportunities to contribute to the community. They serve more than 200000 beneficiaries each year, and include socially isolated seniors, the mentally disadvantaged, and at-risk children from low-income families. They also run the Volunteer Learning Centre, where qualified senior volunteers train other volunteers. |
| National Council of Social Service | The National Council of Social Service develop resources to enhance the capabilities of social service agencies. They developed the Volunteer Management Toolkit 2.0 to provide SSAs with guidelines on how to manage volunteers effectively, and have worked with SSAs such as RSVP Singapore to contribute to development of the Toolkit.                                                                                                              |
