# Definition
- Shared values refers to principles and beliefs that are embraced and seen as important by a group of people. It is shaped by a country's history, heritage and experiences and may be changed over time due to challenges faced by citizens.
# Examples
| Case Study       | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2003 SARS Crisis | During the SARS crisis, Singaporeans observed the request to monitor their own temperatures and wash their hands frequently. Those who were quarantined also complied with the restriction. Neighbours also helped other neighbours who were unable to help themselves. Many volunteered to take the temperature of visitors to markets, or to help disseminate accurate information about SARS to residents in HDB estates |
