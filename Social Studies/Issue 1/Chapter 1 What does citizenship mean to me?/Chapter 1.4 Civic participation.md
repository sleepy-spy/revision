# Definition
- Civic participation refers to citizens taking part in public affairs which are matters of interest or concern to most people in society. It involves citizens contributing to address societal issues, needs and interests that they are passionate or concerned with.

| Temporary Academic Assistance | In 2020, Mr Andy Teo initiated the Temporary Academic Assistance to connect students with volunteer tutors, who saw the need when schools moved to home-based learning during the COVID-19 pandemic, and enlisted the help of tutors through social media to teach for free. Within a week, 250 people volunteered to be tutors |
| Zero Waste SG                 | Zero Waste Singapore is an NGO leading the drive towards zero waste through education and advocacy. It engages the public, companies, schools and government agencies to discuss the 3Rs in relation to different kinds of waste such as food waste and plastic disposables                                                     |
