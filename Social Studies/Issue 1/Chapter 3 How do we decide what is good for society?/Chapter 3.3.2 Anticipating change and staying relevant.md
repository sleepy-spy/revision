# Definition
- Anticipating change and staying relevant means having a government that is forward-looking and takes sound action early, minimising threats and maximising opportunities for the people of Singapore
# Examples
| Case Studies               | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tackling Rising Sea Levels | In 2019, during the National Day Rally, then PM Lee Hsien Loong spoke about the impact of climate change on Singapore and informed Singaporeans about the government taking early and vital action to prepare the country for rising sea levels caused by climate change. In 2020, a budget of $5 billion was set aside for the Coastal and Flood Protection Fund to help the country meet the significant risks of rising sea levels |
