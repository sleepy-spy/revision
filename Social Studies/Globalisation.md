## Definition
- Globalisation is a process which there is movement of people, goods, services, capital, information and ideas around the world, bringing about interconnections and interdependent relationships among countries
## Dimensions of Globalisation
- Political
- Economic
- Social
- Cultural
- Environment
## Driving Forces of Globalisation
- Ideas and activities in different parts of the world become interconnected, creating interdependencies among countries
- Development in Transportation Technology
- Development in Digital Technology
- Growth of MNCs
