# Definition
- Competition for resources is heightened competition with foreigners over limited resources, such as job opportunities and infrastructure. This competition, whether real or perceived, can lead to tensions, unhappiness and negative experiences among groups of people in the country
# Examples
| Case Study | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rose       | Rose, who worked as a receptionist, lost her job due to downsizing in the company. Being unemployed over six months, she depends on her savings to survive while she seeks a new job. She felt that most foreigners leave their families behind in their countries, and have the upper hand as they can work longer hours, odd hours and on weekends, whereas locals like her need to balance work and home life, making her feel that she cannot compete with foreigners. |
