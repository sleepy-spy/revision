# Definition
- Allows people with different backgrounds, qualifications and experiences to share their knowledge and skills, which can lead to the creation of new ideas, methods and products.
# Examples
| Case Study               | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Thermo Fisher Scientific | Thermo Fisher Scientific is a multinational company with over 2000 employees in Singapore and 80000 employees globally as at 2021. Singaporeans in the company are trained in leadership skills and encouraged to collaborate with employees from different cultures, and with different experiences and expertise. They also collaborate with Singapore-based design firms, universities and research institutions, such as A\*STAR, to support their Research and Development in a wide range of areas, including genetics and cancer research |
