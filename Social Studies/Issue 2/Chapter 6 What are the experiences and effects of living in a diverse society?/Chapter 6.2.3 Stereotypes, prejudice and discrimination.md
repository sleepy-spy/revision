# Definition
- A lack of understanding among different groups of people in a diverse society can result in stereotyping, prejudice and discrimination. This can lead to resentment and strained relationships among different groups of people
# Examples
| Case Study                 | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chinese Man Discriminating | In June 2021, an interracial couple comprising a man of Indian and Filipino-Spanish descent and his girlfriend of Thai-Chinese descent were strolling along Orchard road when a local Chinese man confronted the couple and told them to date within their own races. This made the couple feel embarrassed and hurt, and the Chinese man was eventually fired from his job and was charged in court |
