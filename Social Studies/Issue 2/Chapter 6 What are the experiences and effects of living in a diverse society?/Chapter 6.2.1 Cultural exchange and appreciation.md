# Definition
- Cultural exchanges take place with the sharing of ideas, customs and traditions among people from different backgrounds, leading to deeper understanding and appreciation of diverse cultures and also lead to fusion of cultures

| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dr Iskandar Jalil | Dr Iskandar Jalil is a Singaporean potter whose passion for it was ignited when he was introduced to it while pursuing a scholarship in Japan in the 1970s. Inspired by his time in Japan, Dr Iskandar integrates Japanese and Islamic styles into his pottery. His art is influenced by wabi-sabi, a Japanese approach to life that sees beauty in imperfections and in the passing of time, which is reflected in his work, which showcases natural weathering and imperfections. His pottery is found in many public and private art collections in Singapore and around the world |
