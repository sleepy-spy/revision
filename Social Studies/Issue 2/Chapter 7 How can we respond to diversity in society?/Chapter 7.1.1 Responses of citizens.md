# Definition
- Individuals can respond constructively to promote understanding of cultural differences and strengthen social cohesion. However these bonds may be weakened when individuals are insensitive or disrespectful with regard to socio-cultural differences. Citizens with common interests can also organise themselves into community groups to contribute towards specific issues or needs in society

| 2019 Singapore Grand Prix | During a concert at the 2019 Singapore Grand Prix, a social media influencer uploaded a picture of two Sikh men in white turbans in front of her with the caption, "Two huge obstructions decided to pop out of nowhere." | Welcome In My Backyard    | Welcome In My backyard is a a youth-led ground up campaign founded in April 2020 to raise awareness about the lives of migrant workers, and also aims to promote interactions between migrant workers and the local community. Comprising almost 50 volunteers, WIMBY has organised initiatives such as partnering schools in their Values-in-Action programmes to address issues facing migrant workers |
