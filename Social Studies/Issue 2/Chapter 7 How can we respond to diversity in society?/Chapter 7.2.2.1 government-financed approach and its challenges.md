# Definition
- A government financed approach, like the one in Sweden, is when the government provides substantial subsidies, allowances and opportunities to equip individuals with skills for employment, which ensres citizens, regardless of their background or socio-economic status, have access to local services
# Examples
| Case Studies         | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Healthcare in Sweden | Healthcare services in Sweden, such as hospital care, medicine, dental care, disability support and rehabilitation services are publicly funded. All families are covered by a medical protection scheme, and since 2019, dental care costs are free for local residents up to the age of 23. Dental care from the age of 24 onwards is heavily subsidised |
| High Taxation Rate   | The social benefits provided are possible by the high taxation rate in Sweden. In 2019, the highest income earners in Sweden pay incomes taxes of 57.2 per cent, which is one of the highest in the world. Besides income tax, there is also indirect taxation in the form of Value-Added Tax. In 2019, VAT in Sweden was 25 per cent.                     |
