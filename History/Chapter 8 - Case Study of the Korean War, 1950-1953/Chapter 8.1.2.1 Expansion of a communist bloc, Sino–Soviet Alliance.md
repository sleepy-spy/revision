# Expansion of Communism in China
- US pumped US$2 billion into China to support the GMD nationalists between 1946 and 1949
- GMD government was defeated by December 1949, and they established themselves on the island of Formosa
- China and USSR signed the Sino-Soviet Treaty of Friendship, Alliance and Mutual Assistance, including a cumulative loan of $300 million from the USSR to help China rebuild after the war
