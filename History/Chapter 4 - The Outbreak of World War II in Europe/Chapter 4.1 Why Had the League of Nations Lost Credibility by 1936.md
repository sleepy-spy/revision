# Examples
| Case Study                                                      | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Non-membership of key states such as the USSR and United States | The US Congress refused to ratify the Treaty of Versailles or allow the United States to join the League, while the USSR was not allowed to join because it was communist                                             |
| Kellogg-Briand Pact                                             | The Kellogg-Briand pact was an agreement between 65 nations not to use force to settle disputes in 1928                                                                                                               |
| World Disarmament Conference                                    | In July 1932, Germany put forward proposals for all countries to disarm to their level, and when the Conference failed to agree on this principle of "equality" the Germans walked out and the Conference broke up    |
| Abyssinian Crisis                                               | In October 1935, Mussolini's army launched a full-scale invasion of Abyssinia. Even though the League placed sanctions on Italy, the United States continued supplying oil to Italy, leading to the sanctions failing |
| German Remilitarisation of the Rhineland                        | In the midst of the Abyssinian crisis, German troops marched into the Rhineland and remilitarised the area to prepare for war, which the League did nothing to stop.                                                  |
