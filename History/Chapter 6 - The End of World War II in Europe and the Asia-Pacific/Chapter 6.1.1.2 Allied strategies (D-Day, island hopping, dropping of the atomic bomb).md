# Battle of the Atlantic
- The scale and speed of US industrial production allowed the United States to build more ships than the German U-boats could sink, allowing them to take control of the shipping routes from the Atlantic to Britain and the USSR
# D-Day 1944
- On 6 June 1944, Allied forces landed on the heavily fortified coasts of France's Normandy region
- 130000 Allied troops across the English Channel by sea and 23000 by air
- 13000 Allied aircraft pounded radar installations, rail links and bridges
- Cut off German forces from reinforcements
# Island-Hopping Strategy in Asia-Pacific
- The United States came up with strategy of island hopping, which involved using air attacks and submarines to isolate Japanese-held areas, instead of trying to capture them. The allies would then conquer the poorly defended islands, and further cut off the Japanese supply lines which supplied the army with food, ammunition and other essentials.
