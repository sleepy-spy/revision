# Reorganisation and Resistance
- Officers in the Red Army were given greater freedom and independence to act. 
- When the Germans had advanced in 1942 towards the city of Stalingrad, the Soviets held them in a fierce battle and were eventually able to encircle the Germans, forcing them to surrender.
# Resources and Production
- The Soviets enacted harsh measures to keep up with high demand. Adults in the USSR received no food unless they worked in some way for the war effort.
- The Soviet war production equalled and then surpassed German war production in 1942. 
- The Soviets managed to muster a strong response to Germany capturing their industrial complexes by shifting its major industrial complexes to the Urals, Siberia and Kazakhstan.
# Entry to the Asian-Pacific Theatre of War
- On 8 August 1945, the Soviets declared war against Japan and launched their invasion simultaneously on three fronts of Manchuria. The Soviets defeated the Kwantung Army in Manchuria and then occupied northern Korea.
