| Importance of the United States | 1. Between 1941 and 1945, US factories produced 250000 aircraft, 90000 tanks, 350 naval destroyers, 200 submarines and 5600 merchant ships<br>2. The United States supplied the Allies with food, raw materials, industrial equipment, weapons and ammunition, troops, ships and aircraft<br>3. The United States had control of the shipping routes across the Atlantic to Britain and to the USSR.<br>4. The United States came up with strategy of island hopping, which involved using air attacks and submarines to isolate Japanese-held areas, instead of trying to capture them. The allies would then conquer the poor defended islands, and further cut off the Japanese supply lines which supplied the army with food, ammunition and other essentials.<br>5. The Japanese army was forced to surrender after the United States dropped two atomic bombs on Japan, one in Hiroshima and one in Nagasaki. The bombs made the Japanese realise that continuing to fight might lead to their obliteration, leading to their surrender                                 |
| Importance of Britain           | 1. Britain became a substantial military base for the allies. Many allied troops were stationed in Britain, particularly from Canada and the Caribbean. Once the United States entered the war in 1941, Britain became a base for the US army. The British military bases allowed for the D-Day landings of 1944 and allied bombing campaigns against Germany possible<br>2. The British Empire forces also included the immense manpower and resources of India. | Importance of the USSR          | 1. When German advance was halted by Russian winter in 1941, it gave Stalin and the military leaders time to rebuild the Soviet Red Army. When the Germans advanced towards Stalingrad, the Soviets fiercely battled them and eventually managed to encircle them, forcing them to surrender. The victory raised the morale of the Soviets as they began to drive the Germans back<br>2. The adults in the USSR received no food unless they worked for the war effort. The secret police was used to keep control of the population. The harsh measured allowed the USSR to surpass German war production in 1942, and by 1943, the USSR produced one and a half times as many aircraft and twice as many tanks as the Germans.<br>3. The USSR entered the war against Japan three months after the defeat of Germany. On 8 August 1945, the Soviets declared war against Japan and invaded three fronts of Manchuria simultaneously, and they were able to defeat the Kwantun army and occupy northern Korea, contributing to Japan's decision to surrender to allied forces |
