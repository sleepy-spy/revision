# Europe in Ruins
- 36 million Europeans killed
- Much of infrastructure of roads, railways, bridged and canals destroyed by fighting or bombing
- No gas, electricity or running water in many areas
- Widespread disease and malnutrition
- 50 million people lost their homes or had to leave their homes
# Decline of Western European Power
- Britain exhausted of people, industries and empire and became virtually bankrupt
- France no longer being power after being liberated from Germany in 1944
- Germany in ruins after being defeated in WWII
