# Competing Ideologies

## United States
- Ideology: Democracy
	- Vision
		- Individuals have right to choose government
		- Individual liberty in areas such as freedom or speech, religion and assembly
		- Right to own protected property
	- Control:
		- Government chosen in free elections
	- Wealth
		- Created through free market, resulting in great inequality
		- Capitalist with free economy
		- Privately owned businesses and property
	- Individual Interest
		- Valued Individual Freedoms
## USSR
- Ideology: Communism
	- Vision
		- Destroy capitalism through class struggle
		- Create a utopian world where all people are equal, all property and work shared equally among all
	- Control
		- One-party dictatorship
		- Elections held, but all candidates belonged to the communist party
	- Wealth
		- Shared through state control, resulting in low equality and unemployment
		- Communist with command economy
		- "To each according to his ability, for each according to his need"
		- Government controlled all resources to produce goods, including factories and farms
# History of Suspicion and Mistrust before 1939
- USSR communist vision aligned to Communist Manifesto, calling for worldwide communist revolutions to overthrow capitalist states
- During the Paris Peace Conference, Germany not broken up because its a barrier against communism
- 1918-1921: Civil war in Russia between communists and the Whites. US and Britain supported whites
- 1920s: Red Scare in United States where US government arrested and imprisoned immigrants from Russia accusing them of communism, even though they were innocent
- 1930s: British and French had policy of appeasement, hoping Hitler's Germany would oppose communism. USSR fears policy of appeasement threatened its security
- August 1939: Nazi-Soviet Non-Aggression Pact with Germany promising not to attack each other. Surprised US and world because USSR wanted to form an alliance with Britain and France against Hitler, though talks failed
