# Public Sentiments
- British, French and US People felt Germany was responsible and should be punished
- Especially strong in Britain and France, as total casualties on both sides exceeded nine million
- Shortage of food and medicine, villages and areas of Belgium and France devastated
# The Treaty of Brest-Litovsk
- Russia forced Russia to sign the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk in March 1918
- Forced Russia to give Germany large areas of prime agricultural land, industrial areas and much of its coal mines
- Lost a third of its population
- Had to pay 6 billion marks in reparations to Germany
# Aims of Big Three
## United States
- Wanted to set up an international body called the League of Nations, so disputes can be solved without wars
- Germany should be punished but not destroyed completely, so Germany would not start another war (foreshadowing)
- Replace German leaders with those committed to democracy and peace
- Self determination of countries, where countries to rule themselves rather than be part of Austro-Hungarian empire
## Britain
- Protect Britain
- Rebuild Britain's economic prosperity
- Maintain the British Empire
## France
- Suffered most amounts of death compared to population of the Big Three - 4%
- Wanted to ensure Germany would never wage war against France again
- Wanted Germany to be broken into smaller states
# Terms of the Treaty of Versailles
## War Guilt Clause
- Article 231: Germany had to accept total blame for starting the war
## Reparations
- 66 billion pounds of reparations paid to the Allies for war damage
# Territory Changes
- Germany lost 10 per cent of its land, 12.5 per cent of its population
- Forbidden to make alliances with Austria to form an Anschluss
## Armed Forces
- Army was limited 100000 men
- Conscription was banned
- No armoured vehicles, submarines or aircraft
- Only six battleships for the navy
- Demilitarisation of the Rhineland
## League of Nations
- Not allowed to join League of Nations
