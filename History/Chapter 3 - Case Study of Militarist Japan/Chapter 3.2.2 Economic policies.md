| Case Study                           | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Japan's Industrialisation            | The militarist government shifted developing consumer industries to heavy industries and chemical production, and increased spending to stimulate Japan's economy, allocating more funds to heavy industries to expand Japan's manufacturing capabilities and prepare for war                                                                        |
| Formation of Cartels                 | The government passed the Principal Industries Control Law in 1931, allowing zaibatsu to form cartels to limit competition and regulate the production and prices of the products they sold. By 1936, the Japanese military introduced the Five-Year Plans setting targets for production in various industries                                      |
| Increased Control of Labour Unions   | The government no longer favoured unions and forced thousands of workers to withdraw from the union of government employees                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Campaign for Economic Revitalisation | The Ministry of Agriculture launched the Campaign for Economic Revitalisation, which stressed the need to revive the spirit of co-operation in rural areas and encouraged farmers to adopt scientific farming methods rather than traditional ones. The government increased public works in the countryside to provide jobs for people living there |
