| Censorship                     | In 1936, the Publications Monitoring Department formed the Information and Propaganda Committee which worked with the Publications Monitoring Department to control the media, remove negative portrayals of the military and Emperor, and promote their militarist ideas. | Increased Control of Education | In 1937, the Ministry of Education published a document titled "Fundamentals of Our Nation Polity" which served as the basis for Japan's education system. It aimed to inculcate Japanese traditional ethics in their students. The Japanese curriculum contained large amounts of physical exercise, military-style drilling and technical lessons to prepare youths for war                                                                                     |
