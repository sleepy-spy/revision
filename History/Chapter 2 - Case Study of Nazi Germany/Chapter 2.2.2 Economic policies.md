# Examples
| Case Study                             | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| German middle-class workers            | The Strength Through Joy initiative gave workers cheap theatre and cinema tickets, among many other benefits. Hitler introduced the Four-Year Plan, aiming to get the German economy ready for war within four years, which reduced unemployment as the need for rapid production created many jobs |
| German farmers                         | Reich Entailed Farm Law made it impossible for banks to take the land of farmers who were in debt and force them out, saving many farmers from losing their farms.                                                                                                                                  |
| German big businesses                  | German big businesses no longer had to worry about trade unions and strikes, and companies liek Volkswagen made massive profits from government contracts                                                                                                                                           |
| German army                            | Hitler spent vast sums on rearmament, brought back conscription and made plans to make Germany a great military power again in return for loyalty to Hitler                                                                                                                                         |
| Communists and other political parties | Hitler passed a law banning all other political parties, and all local councils and state assemblies had to have a Nazi majority                                                                                                                                                                    |
