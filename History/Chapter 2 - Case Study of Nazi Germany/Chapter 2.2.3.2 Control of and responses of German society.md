# Examples
| Case Study                | Example                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SS                        | The SS was primarily responsible for destroying opposition to Nazism and carrying out the racial policies of the Nazis, and also monitored political opponents and stopped spies                     |
| Propaganda and Censorship | Posters and portraits of Hitler and the Nazi party were throughout Germany to brainwash the German people                                                                                            |
| Anti-Nazi Youth Groups    | Youth groups such as the White Rose encouraged others to resist and reject Nazi rule, and distributed leaflets that revealed the atrocities of the Nazi regime                                       |
| Military Resistance       | Many aristocratic officers and conservatives were worried about Hitler and his foreign policy, and some were involved in assassination attempts against Hitler liked the failed July 1944 Bomb Plot. |
