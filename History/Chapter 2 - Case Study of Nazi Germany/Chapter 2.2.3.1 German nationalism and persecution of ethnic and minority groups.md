# Examples

| Case Study                                          | Example                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| German youths                                       | Many youths joined institutions like the Hitler Youth, which was a paramilitary organisation to groom German youths to be future leaders in Nazi organisations      |
| German women                                        | Many young girls in Germany received vigorous training through the League of German Girls in domestic tasks                                                         |
| Jews                                                | The Nuremberg Laws took away German citizenship from the Jews and deprived them of all civil and political rights, and were forbidden to marry pure-blooded Germans |
| Minority groups (such as the Roma) and the disabled | The Nazis used euthanasia and sterilisation on members of other races and minority groups such as the Roma and mentally ill people                                  |
