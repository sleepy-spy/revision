| Weak constitution of the weimar republic                   | Proportional representation mean that any party in the elections would gain seats in the Reichstag equal to the number of votes they receives. However, it was very difficult for one party to gain a majority, making it challenging to pass laws. Article 48 allowed the president to rule by decree in times or emergency, but the terms of the article were not well defined, so the president could exploit Article 48 to pass laws without consulting the Reichstag |
| Unpopularity of the Weimar Republic with the German people | During the Kapp Putsch in 1920, Wolfgang Kapp led 5000 Freikorps into Berlin to overthrow the Weimar Republic and return Germany to a more authoritarian system similar to the time of the Kaiser                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
