| Occupation of the Ruhr and Hyperinflation | When Germany failed to make a payment of the 6.6 billion pounds in reparations in January 1923, French and Belgian troops occupied the Ruhr valley, Germany's most important industrial region. This halted production, while the government continued printing money, leading to hyperinflation |
| The Great Depression                      | The German Chancellor, Heinrich Bruning, cut the wages of civil servants and most other government employees, and also got Hindenburg to use his emergency powers to pass them into law because the Reichstag objected                                                                           |
| High unemployment rate in the early 1930s | By 1932, 40 per cent of the German workforce was unemployed, and the income of the average German fell by 40 per cent between 1929 and 1932.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Fear of Communism                         | Many business leaders feared the communists because of their plans for state control of businesses, like in the USSR. Those in the middle class were afraid because they also knew communists were hostile to people like them                                                                   |
