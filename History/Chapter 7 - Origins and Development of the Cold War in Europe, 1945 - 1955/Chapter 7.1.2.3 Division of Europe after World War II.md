# The Potsdam Conference
## Change in Leadership
- Truman replaced Roosevelt, and was concerned that USSR posed threat to Western Europe security
- Churchill lost the general election in July 1945 and was replaced with Clement Attlee
- Both Truman and Attlee were younger and more inexperienced in foreign affairs
## Increasing Soviet Control
- Red Army liberated the countries from the Germans, but left their troops there. Countries like Poland were now controlled by the Soviets.
- Stalin set up a communist government in Poland and ignored the wishes of the majority of the Poles
- Encouraged other communists in other countries states to take power
## Manhatten Project
- United States secretly developed atomic bomb, and kept it from the USSR until the Potsdam conference
- Truman informed Stalin about the bomb, but he was not surprised because the Manhattan project had been leaked to the USSR
## Major Disagreements
### Germany
- Stalin wanted to cripple Germany; Truman did not want to repeat Treaty of Versailles mistake
- Stalin wanted compensation; Truman agreed at first, then changed his mind. Stalin thought Truman protect Germany, became suspicious
### Eastern Europe
- Yalta Conference, agree that Eastern Europe would be Soviet sphere of influence
- Stalin thought he could set up communist governments that are loyal to him
- Truman thought Stalin wanted to control Europe
# Worsening Relations after Potsdam
## The Iron Curtain and Soviet Control of Eastern Europe
- Jan Masaryk, a democratic politician, got his phone tapped and got followed by Soviet agents. In September 1947, they tried to kill him with a bomb in a parcel. In March 1948, he was found dead below his bedroom window
- Churchill gave a speech in March 1946, warning about the dangers of the USSR and described the USSR's influence as an "Iron Curtain" across Europe
