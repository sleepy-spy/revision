# Truman Doctrine
- Truman announced in a speech that US would provide resources to democratic countries perceived to be threatened by communist influences
# Developments in Turkey and Greece
- In 1946, civil war between the communists and royalists in Greece. 
- US Congress passed the Greece and Turkey Aid Bill, which was US$400 million in aid to Greece and Turkey, making Turkey a US ally and propped up the monarchist government
# Marshall Plan
- The United States would put $13 billion into helping Europe's crippled economies to recover.
- From April 1948, the US would provide resources like food and countless other items to democratic counties in Europe
# Creation of NATO, 
- Western powers created NATO in 1949 and signed an agreement, and the most important part was that NATO members agreed to go to war if any member was attacked
