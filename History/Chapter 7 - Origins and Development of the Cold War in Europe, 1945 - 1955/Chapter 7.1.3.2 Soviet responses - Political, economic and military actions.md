# Cominform
- The Cominform was set up in 1947 and published propaganda to unite the communist states in Europe, ensure satellite states implement Soviet-style communism
# Comecon
- Comecon was set up so that Comecon members would trade mostly with one another rather than the West, and the satellite states were promised benefits similar to the Marshall plan
# Berlin Blockade
- In June 1948, the USSR cut the rail and road links from the allied zones to West Berlin and disrupted water and power supplies
- Truman ordered the Berlin Airlift, where the United States and its allies supplied West Berlin with coal, food and medicine by air
# Creation of East Germany, 1949
- British, French and US zones would be united to form West Germany
- Soviet zone would become East Germany
- Berlin was also divided into East and West Berlin, and would be divided by the Berlin Wall later in 1961
# Warsaw Pact, 1955
- USSR created the Warsaw Pact in May 1955, where all members agreed if one state was threatened, they would help to defend it, in response to West Germany joining NATO
