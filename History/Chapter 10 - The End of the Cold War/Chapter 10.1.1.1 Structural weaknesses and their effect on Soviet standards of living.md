# Structural Weaknesses of the Command Economy
- The command economy of the Soviet Union had structural weaknesses. Wen the Soviet economy grew and expanded by the 1970,s, the Soviet economy became overwhelmed and struggled to cope with many companies making different goods, causing them to act slowly and make poor decisions
# Poor Standard of Living
- The Soviet people knew that ordinary Americans had cars, telephones, televisions and other devices. While in the USSR, such items were rare and impossible to afford
- Worker's wages also did not increase, and many other non-communist countries had higher living standards than the USSR
# Disillusionment of the Workforce
- Many workers found that their bosses were appointed due to personal connections or bribes
- Wages were also tightly controlled, limiting incentives to work hard, with those protesting or complaining risked being arrested by the secret police
