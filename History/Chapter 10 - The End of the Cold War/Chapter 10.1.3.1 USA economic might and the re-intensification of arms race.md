# Economic Boom in the United States
- The US president, Ronald Reagan, and close ally, British Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher, emphasised cutting government taxes, incentivising people to start businesses and work harder since the government would not be taking their wealth
- Also advocated that governments should spend less and limit their involvement in people's lives as far as possible
- These policies contributed to an economic boom
# Expanding US Military Might
- In Reagan's first two weeks in office, he increased the US defence budget by $32.6 billion, introducing weapons systems like the B1 nuclear bomber
- Reagan gave the go-ahead for the Strategic Defense Initiative, which was a multi-billion-dollar project to create a system that could use satellites and lasers to destroy missiles before they hit their target
- Reagan spent a huge amount on weapons, leaving the US with $4 trillion of debt by the 1980s
