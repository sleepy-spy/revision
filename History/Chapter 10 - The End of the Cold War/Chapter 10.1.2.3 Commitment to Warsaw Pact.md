# Brezhnev Doctrine
- Under the Brezhnev Doctrine, all Eastern Bloc countries would be one-party states, led by the Communist Party
- Any attempts to reverse communism would be seen as a threat to all communist states, and other communist states would intervene by sending in troops
## East Germany
- East Germany's leader, Erich Honecker and was utterly committed to communist rule, and saw the smallest criticism or opposition as a US-inspired plot.
- East Germany's secret police, the Stasi, were known and feared for their harshness and efficiency
