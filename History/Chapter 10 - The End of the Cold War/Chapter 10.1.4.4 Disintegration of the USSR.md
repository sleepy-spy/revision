# Declarations of Sovereignty
- In 1990, many countries of the USSR like Lithuania, Ukraine and Azerbaijan declared independent from the USSR
# Attempted Coup
- Gorbachev's Prime Minister, Pavlov, and head of the armed forces, Dmitry Yazov, held Gorbachev prisoner in his holiday home in Crimea while sending tanks and troops onto the streets
- Crowds gathered in Moscow to strongly oppose the coup, and faced with resistance lead by the popular opposition with its leader, Boris Yeltsin. The plotters lost faith in themselves and the coup collapsed
# Gorbachev's Resignation and the End of the Cold War
- After the coup, Gorbachev returned to Moscow. On 25 December 1991, he made a televised speech to announce his resignation as the president of the USSR and the end of the USSR
- Thus, the Cold War ended.
