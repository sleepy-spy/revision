# Sinatra Doctrine
- In July 1988, Gorbachev stated his intention to all the members of the Warsaw Pact to withdraw Soviet troops, tanks and aircraft from other communist states
- This led to sudden collapse of communism across Eastern Europe. The Berlin Wall, a symbol of the Cold War, fell in November 1989 when the guards abandoned it and crowds dismantled it
# Hungary
- Dismantled barbed wire fence between Hungary and non-communist Austria in 1989
- Declared itself a democratic republic rather than a communist republic after Gorbachev's speech in October 1989 to Eastern European leaders to reform their countries
# Poland
- Solidarity won 99 out of 100 seats in Poland's senate
- Protests against communism continued to take place throughout the summer of 1989
# East Germany
- In September 1989, thousands of East Germans on holiday in Hungary and Czechoslovakia refused to go home and escaped through Austria to West Germany
- One million people demanded for democracy and free elections in East Berlin in 1989
# Czechoslovakia
- In 1989, huge demonstrations forced communist leaders to resign, resulting in the opening of borders and free elections in June 1990
# Bulgaria and Romania
- Massive demonstrations led to the collapse of communist regimes in Bulgaria and Romania
