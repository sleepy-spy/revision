# Ageing Leadership
- By 1974, the average age of Politburo members was 65, which was the mandatory retirement age in many occupations
- When Brezhnev died in 1982, he was succeeded by Yuri Andropov, who only lived till early 1984, and the Soviet leaders were ill-equipped to deal with the ongoing economic stagnation
# Glasnost
- Glasnost allowed open political debate by Communist Party members who disagreed with the government, and also allowed criticisms from the media
- Citizens were exposed to the better quality of life of the outside world, such as the United States and Western European countries
- Backfired as it released much resentment against the communist government, exposing shortcomings of Soviet officials such as inefficiency and corruption
