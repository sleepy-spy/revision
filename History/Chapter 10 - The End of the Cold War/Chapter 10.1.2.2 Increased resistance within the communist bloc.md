# Hungarian Revolution
- In 1956, there were widespread protests in Hungary against the communist regime under nationalist Imre Nagy, announcing that Hungary would withdraw from the Warsaw Pact and hold free elections
- Soviet and tanks invaded Hungary, crushed the revolution and overthrew Nagy's government
# Prague Spring
- The Prague Spring was a period of liberal reforms in Czechoslovakia under the newly elected communist leader Alexander Dubcek. 
- Met with the same fate as the Hungarian revolution, where other members of the Warsaw Pact, led by the USSR, invaded Czechoslovakia and crushed the new government
# Poland and Solidarity
- There were rising food prices which led to growing wave of strikes in Poland during the 1980s, leading to many independent trade unions being formed. The trade unions merged and formed an independent trade union, Solidarity, and gained increasing popularity in Poland
- Solidarity led a series of controlled strikes to push for economic reforms and free elections
- The Civilian Prime Minister was forced to resign, replaced by General Jaruzelski
- Jaruzelski introduced martial law, and placed around 10000 Solidarity members in prison and banned the trade union
