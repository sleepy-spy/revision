# An unpopular and corrupted regime
- Diem rejected land reforms to redistribute land to poorer peasants, because most of his supporters were from the landlord class. The land reforms that were carried out were not far-reaching enough to benefit most peasants, and many landlords clung to their wealth
- Diem practiced nepotism by appointing his brothers as key officials and diplomats.
- Some Buddhist monks protested by setting themselves on fire, and the graphic images made Diem even more unpopular among the people
# South Vietnam Insurgency, 1954 - 1960
- Diem responded to former Viet Minh members' disruption and harassment of regime harshly, locking thousands of suspected communists up and torturing and executing many of them.
- The North Vietnam Communist Party approved Group 559, which transported weapons, ammunition and other equipment to the Viet Cong.
- The Viet Cong carried out around 1700 assassinations between 1957 and 1960, which could be on anyone associated with the South Vietnamese government like police officers and officers of Law Courts
