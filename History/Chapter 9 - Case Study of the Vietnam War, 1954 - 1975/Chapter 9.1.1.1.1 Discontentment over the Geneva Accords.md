# Dien Bien Phu, 1954
- The well-armed force of French troops was thoroughly defeated by the Viet Min, and they were able to cut off French army's supply lines, depriving them of food, medical supplies and ammunition
# The Geneva Conference, 1954
- The French government and public lost interest in retaining Vietnam as a colony. 
- The Viet Minh demanded the recognition of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam and the removal of French troops.
- Vietnam was divided into two along the 17th parallel, with the communist-dominated North under Ho Chi Minh and the non-communist South under Ngo Dinh Diem
# North Vietnam in the 1950s
- Ho Chi Minh introduced the land reform policy to redistribute land to peasant farmers. While met with popularity among peasant farmers, it led to decline in agricultural output. Along with decline in imported foodstuff, it led to famines in North Vietnam
