## LED vs Incandescent
- Benefits of LEDs
	- LEDs are more energy efficient
	- LEDs have a longer lifespan
- Drawbacks of LEDs
	- LEDs cost more than incandescent light bulbs
## Connection
- LEDs should be connected in **forward bias** for current to flow through and it to light up, and **reverse bias** if we want it to not light up
- They should be connected in series with a resistor to limit the current through the LED to prevent it from getting damaged
