- Voltage gain ($A_v$) is the ratio of an amplifier’s output voltage to its input voltage.
- $A_v$ can be calculated using the equation
$$
A_v = \frac{V_{\mathrm{out}}}{V_{\mathrm{in}}}
$$
- Where
	- $A_v$ = voltage gain (no unit)
	- $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ = output voltage in Volt (V)
	- $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ = input voltage in Volt (V)
