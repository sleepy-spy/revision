- A system is a collection of parts that work together to perform one or more functions. 
	- For example, our digestive system consists of different organs that work together to digest food.
- An electronic system is a collection of electronics, mechanical and/or electromechanical parts that work together to perform one or more functions. 
	- For example, an electronic calculator consists of a processor, buttons and a LCD panel to perform  mathematical calculations and shows the results.
