# Definition

| Resistivity                                                                                                                                | Resistance                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Resistivity, $\rho$, is the property of the **material** that affects how well it opposes electricity. Its SI unit is ohm-meter ($\ohm m$) | Resistance is the property that affects how well a **particular conductor or component** opposes electricity. Its SI unit is ohm ($\ohm$) |
