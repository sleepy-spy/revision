# Definition

| Resistivity                                                                                                                                | Resistance                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Resistivity, $\rho$, is the property of the **material** that affects how well it opposes electricity. Its SI unit is ohm-meter ($\ohm m$) | Resistance is the property of a **particular conductor or component** that affects how well it opposes electricity. Its SI unit is ohm ($\ohm$) |
