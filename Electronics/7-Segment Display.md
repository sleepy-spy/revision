## Purpose
- It is used to display a decimal digit
## Common Configurations
- Common-anode
	- The anodes of all of the LEDs are connected, and the users should connect the anodes to a positive voltage source
	- Connect the terminals of the LEDs we want to light up to ground through a resistor
- Common-cathode
	-  The cathodes of all of the LEDs are connected, and users should be connect the cathodes to ground
	- Connect the terminals of the LEDs we want to light up to a positive voltage source through a resistor
